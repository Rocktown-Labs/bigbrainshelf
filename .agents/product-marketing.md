# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-10-31

## 1. Product Overview
- **One-line description:** Picture, coloring, and audiobooks on tech, money, and courage, told so curious kids ages 4 to 8 (and growing up to 18) can actually get it.
- **What it does:** Big Brain Shelf publishes original illustrated storybooks, companion coloring editions, full-color digital editions, and studio-voiced audiobooks. Families can buy individual books (Digital Bundle, Printed Book, Gift Set, or Audiobook) or join The Book Club (quarterly physical Gift Set + back-catalog book) or The Audio Club (monthly/annual unlimited listening).
- **Product category:** Children's educational storybooks, audiobooks, and quarterly book club boxes.
- **Business model & pricing:**
  - Digital Bundle: $9.99 (Story PDF + Coloring PDF + EPUB + Full Color + Online Reader)
  - Audiobook Edition: $6.99 (M4B download + streaming player)
  - Printed Book: $19.99
  - Gift Set: $29.99 (Printed Storybook + Printed Coloring Book + Full Digital & Audio Bundle)
  - The Book Club: $24.99 every 3 months or $89/year
  - The Audio Club: $5.99/month or $49/year

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary Buyer:** Parents, grandparents, and gift-givers shopping for curious kids ages 4–8 (expanding to 9–12 and 13–18).
- **End User:** Kids ages 4–8 who love being read to, listening at bedtime or in the car, flipping through full-color illustrations, and coloring the cast.
- **Jobs to Be Done:**
  1. Introduce real-world ideas (how computers think, saving & earning, speaking up, science, history, math) through warm character stories instead of dry textbooks or noisy screen apps.
  2. Give kids a calm, screen-light bedtime or afternoon ritual with a read-along audiobook player that turns itself off after 60 minutes.
  3. Keep physical books arriving on a predictable quarterly rhythm without duplicate titles.

## 3. Current & Future Curriculum Topics
- **Live Shelves:** Tech, Money, Courage
- **Future Shelves (tracked via Parent Onboarding demand signals):**
  - Science & Nature
  - History & Biographies
  - Math & Logic Puzzles
  - Space & Engineering
  - Art & Music
  - Entrepreneurship & Makers
  - Emotional Intelligence & Kindness

## 4. Brand Voice & Copy Rules
- **Warm, plain, and specific:** Write like a thoughtful parent and bookmaker talking to another parent.
- **No AI tells:** Never use contrast reveals ("It's not X, it's Y"), negation lists, or em dashes in short copy/emails.
- **Ownership clarity (The Lock Model):** Books a family buys are theirs to download and keep forever. Club membership unlocks streaming/reading for the whole catalog while active.

## 5. Key Growth, Onboarding & Referral Loops
- **Parent Onboarding:** Uses Endowed Progress (starts at 25% on account creation). Captures parent name, TOS acceptance, marketing opt-in, current & future learning topics (Science, History, Math, etc.), child's name, age band, character buddy, birthday (for the annual Birthday Gift Email), and bedtime sleep timer / daily limits.
- **Family Referral Loop:** Double-sided "$10 / $10" family referral link inside `/account` ("Give a friend $10 toward their first Gift Set, get $10 shelf credit when they order").
- **Read-Along Theater Mode:** Full-color storybook stacked directly above the audio player with automatic page-turning synced to the narrator and a 60-minute bedtime sleep timer.
